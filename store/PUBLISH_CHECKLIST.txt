VOLTICPLAYER — PUBLISH CHECKLIST

1. Host website files (required for store privacy URLs)
   Deploy the web/ folder (or at least these paths) to https://volticplayer.app :
   - /  (index.html)
   - /privacy/
   - /terms/
   - /faq/
   - /video-downloader/
   - /sitemap.xml
   - /robots.txt
   - /.well-known/assetlinks.json   (replace SHA-256 fingerprint)
   - /.well-known/apple-app-site-association  (replace TEAMID)

2. Google Play Console
   - Paste store/google_play/title.txt, short_description.txt, full_description.txt
   - Category: Video Players & Editors
   - Upload screenshots using store/visuals/screenshot_strategy.txt
   - Feature graphic 1024x500
   - Data Safety: declare media files (on device), optional Google Sign-In, AdMob if used
   - Replace AdMob TEST ids with production ids
   - Create release keystore and sign AAB
   - Update assetlinks.json with release SHA-256

3. App Store Connect
   - Paste store/app_store name, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional_text, whats_new
   - Categories: Photo & Video + Music
   - Privacy Nutrition Labels matching Privacy Policy
   - Replace TEAMID in apple-app-site-association
   - After app is created, put real App Store ID into AppConstants.appStoreUrl and ReviewService.openStoreListing

4. Before submit
   - flutter build appbundle (Android) / archive in Xcode (iOS)
   - Do NOT ship AdMob test IDs
   - Test privacy and terms URLs open in browser
   - Verify package/bundle: com.volticplayer.app on both platforms
