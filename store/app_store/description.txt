VolticPlayer helps you play local and online video and music in one modern app.

Enjoy smooth playback with playlists, equalizer, subtitles, picture-in-picture, background audio, favorites, history, and tools for supported public media downloads.

Features
• Video and music player
• Folders, playlists, and smart playlists
• Equalizer and audio effects
• Subtitles and gesture controls
• Online search and download manager for authorized public media
• Network streams and IPTV

Only download content you have permission to save and use.
