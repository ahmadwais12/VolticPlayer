FAQ

What is VolticPlayer?

A modern player for local and online video and music, with playlists, equalizer, subtitles, PiP, and tools for supported public media downloads.

Can I download any video online?

No. Only download content you have the right to save. The app supports public HTTPS media and openly available formats from sources that provide them.

Does it work offline?

Yes for local files and finished downloads. Online search and streaming need a network connection.
