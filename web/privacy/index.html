Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VolticPlayer (“we”, “the app”) handles information when you use our Android or iOS application and related website pages at volticplayer.app.

1. Who we are

VolticPlayer is a media player for local and online video and audio. Contact: support@volticplayer.app.

2. Information we process

Depending on how you use the app, we may process:

  • Media files on your device — video, audio, and related metadata you choose to open, import, or play. These stay on your device unless you share or upload them yourself.
  • App preferences — settings such as theme, playback options, and download preferences stored locally.
  • Network activity — when you stream, search online media, download supported public media URLs, or open links, standard network data (such as IP address) is processed by your network and the services you contact.
  • Account information (optional) — if you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile details Google provides (for example name, email, profile photo) for the Profile feature.
  • Advertising identifiers — if ads are enabled with Google Mobile Ads / AdMob, Google may process advertising identifiers and device/app interaction data according to Google’s policies. Production builds should use your own AdMob configuration and consent flows where required.
  • Diagnostics (optional) — if you enable anonymous analytics in Settings, limited usage events may be stored for improving stability. Today this may be local-only until a privacy-compliant analytics backend is configured.

3. Permissions

  • Photos / media / storage — to browse and play your library.
  • Notifications — for media playback controls while audio continues in the background.
  • Internet / network state — for streaming, online search, downloads of supported URLs, and updates.
  • Local network (iOS) — to discover NAS / DLNA / UPnP devices when you use those features.

4. How we use information

  • Provide playback, library, playlist, subtitle, equalizer, and download features
  • Remember your preferences
  • Show optional ads (when configured)
  • Respond to support requests you send us
  • Improve reliability and user experience

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be processed by service providers you interact with through the app, such as:

  • Google (Sign-In, AdMob) when those features are used
  • Third-party media hosts or APIs when you search or open online content
  • Apple / Google as platform providers

6. Downloads and online content

Download and online tools are intended for content you have the right to save and use. You are responsible for complying with copyright and the terms of any source you access.

7. Children’s privacy

VolticPlayer is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). Do not provide personal information if you are under that age.

8. Data retention

Local library data, downloads, and settings remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Support emails are retained as needed to respond to you.

9. Your choices

  • Revoke media, notification, or photo permissions in system settings
  • Sign out of Google from the Profile screen
  • Disable optional analytics in Settings when available
  • Uninstall the app to remove local app data

10. International users

If you access the app from outside your home country, your information may be processed in other countries where service providers operate, subject to applicable law.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@volticplayer.app
